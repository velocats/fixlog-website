Simple CMMS alternative

Maintenance software for operations that are too small for a CMMS.

A CMMS assumes a maintenance department: technicians, work orders, permissions, an implementation. Plenty of shops, kitchens, clinics, studios, and rental operations need the records without any of that. FixLog is the middle option between a spreadsheet and a platform.

Where small operations actually land

A spreadsheet is too little. A CMMS is too much.

Spreadsheet

Holds rows. Does not hold photos, warranty PDFs, QR labels, reminders that fire, or a report you can hand someone.

Reminder app

Tells you something is due, then forgets it ever happened. No history, no cost, no documents.

Enterprise CMMS

Work orders, technician assignment, permissions, seats, onboarding. Genuinely the right answer for a maintenance team, and heavy for a five-person business.

FixLog: asset records, recurring reminders, service history, costs, photos, documents, warranties, QR labels, reports, exports, and Spaces on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No account. No seats. $7.99 once if you want it forever.

What you give up, stated plainly.

Worth knowing before you download, so you do not find out later.

No user accounts or permissions

FixLog does not require an account to use, which also means there are no logins, roles, or per-user audit trails. Records live on your device and sync across your own supported devices.

No work-order assignment

You log work that was done and schedule work that is due. Dispatching jobs to a team of technicians is a different kind of product.

Apple platforms only

iPhone, iPad, and Mac. There is no web app and no Android app.

No cloud dashboard

Nothing to administer, nothing to log into, and no vendor holding your maintenance records. Exports and backups are yours to move.
