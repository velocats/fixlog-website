Support

Need help with FixLog?

Email support and include what you were trying to do, what happened, your app version, and whether you were working with real or example data.

Contact

Email support@fixlogapp.com, or use the form below to open a pre-addressed email.

Email Support Open Blank Email

If your browser does not open an email app, email support@fixlogapp.com directly and paste your message.

Helpful Details

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • Your FixLog version
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Screenshots if they help explain the problem
  • Whether the issue involves assets, reminders, QR codes, backups, exports, or Pro unlock

Before you write

For data questions, mention whether you are asking about assets, maintenance history, backups, CSV exports, documents, or QR labels.
