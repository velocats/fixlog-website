Frequently asked questions.

Does FixLog require an account?

No. FixLog works entirely on your device without an account. Your data stays local unless you export or back it up yourself.

Can I track my home and business in the same app?

Yes. Create a separate Space for each — your home, your business, a rental property, a cabin, whatever you need. Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, documents, and history completely separate. Learn how Spaces work.

Where should I start?

Create your first Space, add a few important assets, then create reminders for the maintenance work you already repeat. The example data gives you a fully populated reference to see how it all fits together.

Can I keep copies of my records?

Yes. Use the export and backup tools in the app to keep copies of your data outside of FixLog.

Can I attach manuals, warranties, or receipts?

Yes. FixLog supports photos and PDFs attached directly to asset records — manuals, invoices, inspection forms, warranty cards, and more.

Is FixLog Pro a subscription?

No. FixLog Pro is a one-time $39.99 in-app purchase. You pay once and it stays unlocked, including across all your Spaces.

What if I get a new iPhone?

Use Restore Purchase in Settings to recover your Pro unlock. Use FixLog's backup export to move your data to the new device.
