Equipment repair log

An equipment repair log for people who maintain real machines.

Compressors, tanks, hoods, dock doors, pumps, vans, benches, safety equipment. FixLog gives each one an asset record with its IDs, warranty, documents, service history, and a printable QR label so the record opens while you are standing in front of the machine.

See QR labels

Why equipment records go stale

The record is in the office. The equipment is not.

Maintenance notes get written down when someone gets back to a desk, which usually means they get written down later, or not at all. Scanning a label on the machine removes that gap.

Print a QR label per asset

Stick it on the compressor, the hood, the panel, the trailer. Scanning opens that asset's record: history, reminders, documents, and the button to add a new entry.

Useful when the name is not obvious

In an equipment room with four similar units, "which one is Brite Tank #1" is a real question. The label answers it, which matters most for whoever is covering a shift.

The asset record

The details you only want to look up once.

Every asset holds name, asset ID, status, place, location, manufacturer, model number, serial number, vendor, purchase date, purchase cost, replacement cost, notes, warranty details with an attached PDF, and custom fields for whatever your equipment needs — capacity, voltage, filter size, tag number.

Built for operations with more than one room.

Spaces keep each site's equipment records separate. Locations and groups organise assets inside a Space, so a tank room, a cellar, and a rear dock do not turn into one long list.

Templates to start from

A 263-asset template library covers common equipment and facility categories with suggested reminders and fields.

Reports for operations

Operations Maintenance Report, Warranty Countdown, Handoff Report, CSV export, and printable QR labels.

Cost and replacement exposure

Purchase cost and replacement cost across the asset list show what it would take to replace what you own. See cost tracking.

What FixLog is not: there are no user accounts, seats, or logins, and no cloud dashboard to administer. If you need multi-technician work-order assignment and permissions, you need a CMMS. If you need dependable equipment records without that overhead, see FixLog as a CMMS alternative.
