Help Guide

Use FixLog for a home, a business, or both.

Spaces keep each home, business, workshop, rental, or property separate while letting you manage everything from one app.

Two separate examples. One app.

Create a Space for each thing you manage. Your home can have its own assets and reminders, while your business keeps a completely separate set of records.

Home Space

Main Home

Use a home Space to remember household systems, appliance repairs, warranties, seasonal work, and recurring maintenance.

1

Add home items

Add assets such as Furnace, Water Heater, Refrigerator, Smoke Detectors, Sprinkler System, Garage Freezer, and Lawn Mower.

2

Organize by place

Use locations like Basement, Kitchen, Garage, Yard, Utility Room, or Upstairs Bathroom.

3

Create reminders

Track filter changes, water heater flushing, gutter cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, smoke detector tests, and seasonal maintenance.

4

Save useful records

Attach receipts, manuals, warranty PDFs, repair photos, paint labels, and contractor notes so they are easy to find later.

Business Space

Example Business

Use a business Space to track equipment, tools, facilities, vehicles, inspections, and repeat service work without mixing it with home data.

1

Add business assets

Add assets such as Glycol Chiller, Boiler System, Canning Line, CO2 Manifold, Taproom Glasswasher, vehicles, or shop equipment.

2

Organize operations

Use locations like Brew House, Taproom, Kitchen, Shop Floor, Vehicle Yard, Storage Room, or Office.

3

Schedule service

Create reminders for preventive maintenance, sanitation checks, inspections, lubrication, safety checks, and vendor service.

4

Keep work history

Record notes, parts, costs, labor time, checklist results, photos, reports, and what needs follow-up next time.

You can use both at the same time. Switch Spaces when you want to move from Main Home to Example Business, Cabin, Rental Property, Workshop, RV, or any other Space you create.

Workflow Diagram

The same workflow works inside each Space. Create assets, add reminders, record work, and keep useful history separate for every home, business, or property.

Daily Use

  • Check the active Space name so you know whether you are viewing your home, business, rental, workshop, or another Space.
  • Open Home to see priority work for the selected Space.
  • Open Due to handle overdue and upcoming reminders.
  • Scan a QR code to jump straight to an asset.
  • Record maintenance as soon as work is finished.
  • Snooze or complete reminders as work changes.

Setup Tips

  • Create separate Spaces for major areas of your life, such as Main Home, Cabin, Rental Property, Workshop, or Business.
  • Use locations for physical places inside each Space.
  • Use groups for flexible watch lists.
  • Use templates for repeatable work.
  • Attach photos and PDFs when they help future troubleshooting.
  • Export backups after major updates to your records.

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for common setup, data, and support questions.

Does FixLog require an account?

No. The core FixLog workflow is designed for local use without requiring an account.

Where should I start?

Create your first Space, add a few important assets, then create reminders for the maintenance work you already repeat.

Can I track my home and business separately?

Yes. Create one Space for your home and another Space for your business. Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, documents, and maintenance history separate.

Can I keep copies of my records?

Yes. Use export and backup tools to keep copies of your asset and maintenance data outside the app.

Can I attach manuals or paperwork?

FixLog supports photos and documents for records such as manuals, invoices, inspection forms, and warranties.

How do I get help?

Email support@fixlogapp.com with your app version, device, and a short description of what happened.
