Common questions.

Clear answers before you download.

Is FixLog Pro a subscription?

FixLog Pro is available as a $0.99/month subscription or a $7.99 lifetime unlock.

Can I try FixLog before paying?

Yes. You can explore the workflow with populated example business data and create a small starter set of your own asset records before unlocking Pro.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks unlimited asset records, QR labels, reports, exports, documents, photos, cost tracking, warranty countdowns, handoff reports, sync across supported devices, and multiple Spaces.

Does FixLog require an account?

No. The core FixLog workflow is designed for local use without requiring an account.

Where is my data stored?

Your records stay on your device unless you choose to export, share, or back them up using the tools available on your device.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. Use Restore Purchase in the app if you reinstall FixLog or move to another device using the same Apple ID.
