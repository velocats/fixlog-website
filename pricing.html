Common questions.

Is FixLog Pro a subscription?

No. FixLog Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. You pay once and the Pro features stay unlocked on your device permanently.

Can I try FixLog before buying?

Yes. The free version includes a fully populated example — Mountain Peak Brewing for business users and Maple Ridge Home for homeowners. You can explore the entire workflow and decide whether FixLog fits before purchasing Pro.

Who is FixLog Pro for?

Anyone who maintains things — homeowners tracking appliances and HVAC, business owners tracking equipment and facilities, rental property owners, workshop operators, and more. The $39.99 price is the same regardless of how you use it.

Does Pro require an account or internet connection?

No account is required. FixLog works entirely on your device. Your data stays local unless you choose to export or back it up yourself.

What happens if I replace my iPhone or iPad?

Use the Restore Purchase option in the app to recover your Pro unlock on a new device using the same Apple ID. Use FixLog's export and backup tools to move your data.

How many assets can I track with the free version?

The free version lets you create a limited number of your own asset records so you can test the workflow before committing. The example data is unlimited and fully functional.

Who do I contact with purchase questions?

Email support@fixlogapp.com with your app version, device, and a short description of the issue.
