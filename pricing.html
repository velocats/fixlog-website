Common pricing questions.

A few quick answers before you buy or restore FixLog Pro.

Is FixLog Pro a subscription?

No. FixLog Pro is planned as a one-time in-app purchase.

Can I try FixLog first?

Yes. The free version lets you explore the core workflow and decide whether the app fits your maintenance setup.

Does Pro require an account?

No account is required for the local app workflow.

What if I replace my phone?

Use the app's restore purchase option for the Pro unlock, and use FixLog backup/export tools to keep copies of your maintenance data.
