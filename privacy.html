Privacy

Privacy Policy

FixLog is designed around local, user-controlled maintenance records.

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Data You Enter

FixLog stores the asset, maintenance, reminder, photo, document, group, location, and backup information you choose to enter in the app.

Local Storage

FixLog is intended to keep your records on your device unless you choose to export, back up, share, print, email, or restore them.

No Account Required

FixLog does not require an account to use the app.

Exports and Backups

When you export CSV files, QR labels, reports, or backup files, you control where those files are saved or shared.

Support Emails

If you email support, FixLog may receive the information you include in that email so support can respond.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@fixlogapp.com.
