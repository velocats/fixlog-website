How It Works

Everything you maintain. One app. Completely organized.

FixLog uses Spaces to keep your home, business, rental property, and cabin totally separate while letting you switch between them in a single tap.

Step 1

Open Settings

Tap Manage Spaces to see and create your Spaces.

Step 2

You start with one

Your first Space is ready. Add assets, reminders, and history here.

Step 3

Add another Space

Give it a name and type: Home, Business, Rental, or anything else.

Step 4

Both are separate

Switch between them with a tap. Each Space has its own assets, reminders, history, and records. Nothing mixes.

Spaces

A Space for everything you maintain.

Most people maintain more than one thing. FixLog lets you create a separate Space for each. Your home's service history never mixes with your business records, and your cabin stays organized without cluttering your main home.

Each Space is completely independent. Its own assets, its own reminders, its own maintenance history, its own cost records. Switch between them from the home screen in one tap.

You only need one FixLog Pro purchase to use as many Spaces as you need. Your home and business are covered for the same $39.99.
