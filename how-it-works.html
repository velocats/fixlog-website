Spaces

A Space for everything you maintain.

Most people maintain more than one thing. FixLog lets you create a separate Space for each. Your home's service history never mixes with your business records, and your cabin stays organized without cluttering your main home.

Each Space is completely independent. Its own assets, its own reminders, its own maintenance history, its own cost records. Switch between them from the home screen in one tap.

You only need one FixLog Pro purchase to use as many Spaces as you need. Your home and business are covered for the same $39.99.

What FixLog Tracks

Add assets. Set reminders. Record work. Keep the history.

The same simple workflow works inside every Space, whether you are tracking a home, a business, or both.

Assets

Add every item you maintain: appliances, HVAC, vehicles, equipment, safety systems, facilities. Include photos, serial numbers, warranty dates, purchase cost, and notes.

Reminders

Set recurring maintenance tasks with due dates and recurrence intervals. FixLog shows what is overdue, due today, and coming up. Mark tasks complete and the history is saved automatically.

Maintenance History

Every completed reminder creates a history entry. Add manual records too: what was done, who did it, what it cost, what parts were used, and any notes for next time.

Warranties and Documents

Log warranty expiration dates for every asset. Attach PDFs and photos: manuals, receipts, inspection reports, and warranty cards. Always there when you need them.

QR Code Labels

Generate a QR label for any asset. Stick it on the equipment. Scan it later to jump straight to the service record without searching through lists.

Reports and Exports

View cost tracking charts, warranty countdowns, and maintenance history reports. Export to CSV or generate a handoff report showing everything documented in a Space.
