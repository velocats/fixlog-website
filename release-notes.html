Release Notes

FixLog Updates

See what is new in FixLog, including improvements for Spaces, home and business setup, cost tracking, reminders, assets, storage management, and backups.

1.1.1

Major Home, Business, Cost, and Spaces Update

More sample data, better setup, richer cost tracking, and stronger maintenance workflows.

FixLog 1.1.1 expands the app for both home and business use with separate setup paths, richer sample Spaces, new cost tracking charts, improved reminders, better asset history, storage management, and Pro purchase access from Settings.

Home and business setup

  • Added Home vs Business setup direction with separate sample data paths.
  • Kept the existing Mountain Peak Brewing business sample.
  • Added a new Maple Ridge Home sample for homeowner-focused setup.
  • Expanded both sample sets to 30 assets each.
  • Updated onboarding and help wording around small business use and sample data.

Richer sample data

  • Added richer sample asset details, including locations, groups, vendors, warranty dates, purchase cost, replacement cost, notes, fields, reminders, and maintenance history.
  • Added multi-year sample cost data so charts feel alive.
  • Updated sample cost data so the year chart has data in every month.

Cost tracking

  • Added cost tracking with charts for spend trends, Spaces, locations, vendors, maintenance types, asset value, and replacement exposure.
  • Added dropdown filters in Cost Tracking for Space and then matching Location.

Assets, warranties, and storage

  • Added warranty countdown access through Assets.
  • Added storage management in Settings for large images and documents.
  • Moved maintenance entries higher on the Asset screen.
  • Made long maintenance histories collapsible after the most recent three entries.
  • Added asset support for up to 10 photos, with clearer “save first, then add photos” behavior.

Reminders and backups

  • Improved reminders with visible Complete buttons.
  • Added multi-select complete and delete actions on Due.
  • Reminder completion now logs into maintenance history by default.
  • Added the default FixLog Backup asset and weekly backup reminder.
  • Replaced Home screen “Due Today” with Upcoming, showing the next three non-overdue reminders no matter how far out.

Purchase and polish

  • Added debug and Pro purchase work, including product ID com.quiettools.fixlog.pro.
  • Added purchase access in Settings.
  • Improved several modal and edit screens to use larger presentation sizes.
  • Fixed build issues as they came up, including StoreKit import, CSV actor isolation, asset search logic, and form complexity problems.
1.1

Multi-Space Update

Track multiple spaces from one app.

FixLog 1.1 added Spaces, giving you a clean way to separate different homes, businesses, properties, and projects while keeping everything in one app.

Main Home Cabin Rental Property Workshop RV Small Business

What changed

  • Create and switch between multiple Spaces, such as a home, cabin, rental property, workshop, RV, or business.
  • Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, documents, maintenance history, and records separate.
  • Homeowner onboarding and example data now better fit home and property maintenance.
  • Backups now support multi-space data so your Spaces and related records stay together.
  • App wording has been cleaned up so FixLog feels useful for homes, businesses, workshops, and property owners.
1.0

Initial Release

FixLog 1.0 introduced practical maintenance tracking for small businesses, owner-operated teams, and people who need a better way to remember service history.

Highlights

  • Create asset records for equipment, tools, rooms, vehicles, systems, and facility items.
  • Track maintenance history with type, service date, notes, parts, cost, labor time, and photos.
  • Create maintenance reminders with due dates, recurrence, completion, snooze, and status tracking.
  • Use maintenance templates and checklists for repeatable work.
  • Generate and scan QR codes for assets.
  • Organize assets with locations and flexible groups.
  • Attach photos and PDF documents to assets.
  • Export asset CSV, maintenance CSV, reminder CSV, QR labels, asset reports, and backup files.
  • Explore a fully populated sample company.
  • Unlock Pro with a one-time purchase.
