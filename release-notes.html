Release Notes

FixLog 1.0

The first public release of FixLog focuses on practical maintenance tracking for small businesses and owner-operated teams.

1.0

Initial Release

FixLog 1.0 introduces asset tracking, maintenance logs, reminders, QR codes, templates, groups, locations, documents, exports, and backups.

Highlights

  • Create asset records for equipment, tools, rooms, vehicles, systems, and facility items.
  • Track maintenance history with type, service date, notes, parts, cost, labor time, and photos.
  • Create maintenance reminders with due dates, recurrence, completion, snooze, and status tracking.
  • Use maintenance templates and checklists for repeatable work.
  • Generate and scan QR codes for assets.
  • Organize assets with locations and flexible groups.
  • Attach photos and PDF documents to assets.
  • Export asset CSV, maintenance CSV, reminder CSV, QR labels, asset reports, and backup files.
  • Explore a fully populated sample company.
  • Unlock Pro with a one-time purchase.
