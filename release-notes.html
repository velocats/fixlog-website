1.1

Multi-Space Update

Track multiple spaces from one app.

FixLog 1.1 adds Spaces, giving you a clean way to separate different homes, businesses, properties, and projects while keeping everything in one app.

Main Home Cabin Rental Property Workshop RV Small Business

What changed

  • Create and switch between multiple Spaces, such as a home, cabin, rental property, workshop, RV, or business.
  • Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, documents, maintenance history, and records separate.
  • Homeowner onboarding and example data now better fit home and property maintenance.
  • Backups now support multi-space data so your Spaces and related records stay together.
  • App wording has been cleaned up so FixLog feels useful for homes, businesses, workshops, and property owners.
1.0

Initial Release

FixLog 1.0 introduced practical maintenance tracking for small businesses, owner-operated teams, and people who need a better way to remember service history.

Highlights

  • Create asset records for equipment, tools, rooms, vehicles, systems, and facility items.
  • Track maintenance history with type, service date, notes, parts, cost, labor time, and photos.
  • Create maintenance reminders with due dates, recurrence, completion, snooze, and status tracking.
  • Use maintenance templates and checklists for repeatable work.
  • Generate and scan QR codes for assets.
  • Organize assets with locations and flexible groups.
  • Attach photos and PDF documents to assets.
  • Export asset CSV, maintenance CSV, reminder CSV, QR labels, asset reports, and backup files.
  • Explore a fully populated sample company.
  • Unlock Pro with a one-time purchase.
