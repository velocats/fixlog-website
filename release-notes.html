1.1.1

UX, Templates & Home Screen Update

Redesigned home screen, 263-asset template library, tighter UI across all tabs, and new document features.

Version 1.1.1 is a significant UX update focused on reducing visual noise, surfacing actionable information on the home screen, and making it faster to add assets with an intelligent template system.

Home Screen

  • Replaced the large hero card with a compact tappable status bar: Overdue · Upcoming · Out of Service · Assets.
  • Added Out of Service section. Unavailable assets now surface directly on the home screen.
  • Added Warranty Watch section. Assets with warranties expiring within 90 days are shown sorted by soonest first.
  • Needs Attention now shows 5 reminders, up from 3, without the redundant pill row.
  • Removed the Quick Actions grid. Those actions are already in the toolbar and tab bar.

Asset Templates

  • New template library with 263 asset templates across 21 business types.
  • Business type selector available in Settings and inline in the Add Asset form via a compact picker.
  • Browse Templates sheet is searchable and grouped by category, filtered to the selected business type.
  • ~80 common assets include curated suggested reminders that are editable before saving and created automatically on save.
  • ~50 asset types pre-populate custom fields with labels and placeholder hints.

UI Improvements

  • Assets, Due, and all report screens: FixLog logo added to nav bar title. Empty large-title gaps fixed.
  • FixLog brand card removed from the top of Assets, Due, and all report screens.
  • Assets filters: 4 tall picker rows collapsed into a compact 2×2 chip grid.
  • Due reminder rows: compacted to 2 lines, roughly doubling visible reminders per screen.
  • Add Asset form: Manufacturer and Warranty sections collapse by default.
  • Reports tab: reorganized into Maintenance, Analysis, and Documents sections.
  • Maintenance trend chart: Spend is now green, Completed is purple.
  • "Critical:" prefix stripped from reminder titles in list views. The OVERDUE pill already communicates urgency. Data unchanged.

Documents

  • PDF attachment available directly in the Add Asset form (Pro).
  • "Attach Warranty PDF" button added inside the Warranty section on Asset Detail. Files are categorized as Warranty automatically.
  • Asset rows now show a document count badge when non-photo documents are attached.

Bug Fixes

  • Camera permission button changed from "Allow Camera Access" to "Continue" per App Store Guideline 5.1.1(iv).
  • Demo asset images now load from the bundled SampleAssetImages folder instead of low-quality base64 blobs.
  • Fixed crash when browsing templates caused by a duplicate dictionary key.
  • Replaced iOS 18-only SF Symbols with iOS 17-compatible equivalents.
  • Fixed empty Picker selection warning on first launch.

Home, Business, Cost, and Spaces Update

Home setup path, richer sample data, cost tracking charts, and stronger maintenance workflows.

Added Home vs Business setup direction with separate sample data, expanded sample sets to 30 assets each, cost tracking with charts, warranty countdown, storage management, and Pro purchase access from Settings.

What changed

  • Added Maple Ridge Home sample alongside Mountain Peak Brewing.
  • Cost tracking with charts for spend by Space, location, vendor, type, and time.
  • Multi-select complete and delete on the Due screen.
  • Reminder completion logs to maintenance history by default.
  • Up to 10 photos per asset, storage management in Settings.
  • Warranty Countdown accessible through Assets and Reports.
1.1

Multi-Space Update

Track multiple spaces from one app.

FixLog 1.1 added Spaces, giving you a clean way to separate different homes, businesses, properties, and projects while keeping everything in one app.

Main Home Cabin Rental Property Workshop RV Small Business

What changed

  • Create and switch between multiple Spaces such as a home, cabin, rental property, workshop, RV, or business.
  • Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, documents, maintenance history, and records separate.
  • Backups support multi-space data.
1.0

Initial Release

FixLog 1.0 introduced practical maintenance tracking for small businesses, owner-operated teams, and people who need a better way to remember service history.

Highlights

  • Asset records for equipment, tools, rooms, vehicles, systems, and facility items.
  • Maintenance history with type, service date, notes, parts, cost, and photos.
  • Maintenance reminders with due dates, recurrence, completion, and snooze.
  • Maintenance templates and checklists for repeatable work.
  • QR code generation and scanning.
  • Locations, groups, documents, and CSV export.
  • One-time Pro unlock.
