Maintenance log app

A maintenance log that also tells you what is due.

Most maintenance logs are written after the fact. FixLog handles both halves: recurring reminders and a calendar for the work that is coming, and a permanent record of the work once it is finished. Completing a reminder writes the log entry.

How it works

Two halves of the same record

A schedule you can act on, and a log you can trust.

A reminder app tells you something is due and then forgets it happened. A spreadsheet holds what happened but never warns you. FixLog keeps both connected to the same asset.

Before the work

Set recurring reminders for filter changes, inspections, safety checks, cleanings, calibrations, and seasonal service. The Today screen shows what is overdue, due today, and coming up. The Calendar tab lays the same work out by date.

After the work

Mark the reminder complete and the entry lands in that asset's maintenance history with its date, type, provider, and cost. The next occurrence schedules itself.

What preventive maintenance records are for

Proof that the work actually happened.

A maintenance log earns its keep on the days you need to show your work: an inspection, a warranty claim, an insurance question, a new hire taking over a room full of equipment.

Shop and production equipment

Vehicles and mobile assets
