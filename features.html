Feature List

Everything you need to maintain homes, properties, tools, and equipment.

FixLog combines Spaces, asset records, maintenance history, reminders, QR codes, groups, locations, templates, documents, backups, and exports in one focused app for homes, businesses, workshops, rentals, and more.

Spaces

Create separate Spaces for your Main Home, Cabin, Rental Property, Workshop, RV, or Small Business. Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, documents, and history separate.

Asset Records

Track appliances, tools, vehicles, rooms, home systems, business equipment, and high-value items with photos, status, category, location, vendor, model, serial number, notes, and dates.

Locations

Use locations like Kitchen, Garage, Utility Room, Basement, Workshop, Packaging Area, Truck 2, Taproom Bar, or Storage Room to find items quickly.

Asset Groups

Create flexible groups such as Important Tools, Warranty Watch, Seasonal Equipment, Safety Critical, Appliances, Outdoor Gear, or High-Value Items.

Maintenance History

Log filter changes, inspections, cleaning, preventive maintenance, repairs, replacements, costs, parts, labor time, notes, and photos.

Maintenance Reminders

Track recurring home and business tasks such as HVAC filters, water heater flushing, gutter cleaning, inspections, safety checks, and service work.

Templates and Checklists

Create reusable maintenance templates with checklist steps for repeatable work.

QR Codes

Generate and scan QR codes so you, family members, helpers, or staff can open a record from the item itself. Learn the QR workflow.

Documents

Attach photos and PDF documents such as manuals, receipts, contractor invoices, inspection forms, warranty records, and repair notes.

Reports and Exports

Export asset reports, CSV files, QR labels, and FixLog backup files.

Example Spaces

Explore sample Spaces for home and business use, with assets, reminders, templates, maintenance actions, groups, and locations.

Privacy First

No account is required. Your data stays on your device unless you export or share it.

Flat Price

Unlock FixLog Pro with a one-time purchase. No subscription is required.

Data & Export

Your maintenance records should stay useful.

FixLog is designed around practical record ownership: keep Space-by-Space maintenance history in the app, attach supporting documents, export CSV/report files when needed, and create backup files before major changes or device moves.

Simple Setup

Built for owners, not implementation projects.

Use FixLog for the things you already maintain: your home, cabin, rental property, workshop, vehicles, tools, business equipment, and high-value items. Start small, then add Spaces, templates, QR labels, groups, and recurring reminders as your needs grow.
