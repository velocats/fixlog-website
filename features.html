Features

Everything you need to keep business maintenance records organized.

FixLog connects assets, reminders, service history, service providers, costs, photos, documents, warranties, QR labels, Spaces, sync, and reports so your records stay useful over time.

Asset tracking

Asset records for small business operations.

Track equipment, tools, vehicles, rooms, facility systems, business assets, and high-value items with photos, serial numbers, purchase details, warranty dates, locations, groups, service providers, and notes.

Stay ahead

Recurring reminders and due lists.

Set reminders for seasonal maintenance, inspections, safety checks, filter changes, cleanings, service work, and repeat tasks. Complete a reminder and FixLog saves the history.

Core feature groups.

The features are grouped around the records people actually need later. If you are looking for one specific job, start with the repair log, recurring maintenance, or service history.

Maintenance History

The in-app record of repairs, inspections, service notes, parts used, costs, photos, dates, service providers, and follow-up details for each asset.

Photos and documentation

Attach manuals, receipts, invoices, inspection forms, warranty cards, before-and-after photos, and repair notes to the correct asset.

Cost Tracking

The in-app report for maintenance spend, provider history, repeat repairs, asset value, replacement exposure, and long-term cost trends.

Reports

Operations Maintenance Report, Warranty Countdown, Handoff Report, CSV files, QR labels, and backup files for business records.

Device sync

Keep maintenance records up to date across supported devices. Start a maintenance log on one device and continue from another.

Small business simplicity

Use the core FixLog workflow without a complex rollout, enterprise CMMS setup, or user account system.

Service Provider Directory

Remember who serviced what.

Save the vendors and service providers you rely on, including contact details, specialties, ratings, notes, and past service history, so the right provider is easy to reuse the next time an asset needs work.

Helpful extras that make the system practical.

FixLog includes tools that reduce searching, speed up setup, and make records easier to use later.

Template library

Use asset templates across common small business equipment and facility categories with suggested reminders and useful starting fields.

QR code labels

Print QR labels for assets so you can scan the item itself and open the correct record immediately.

Warranty countdown

See expired and upcoming warranty dates so you can act before a covered repair becomes harder to document.

Custom organization

Use Spaces, locations, groups, categories, statuses, service providers, and notes to match how your operation works.

Exports and backups

Export reports, CSV files, QR labels, and backup files for documentation, planning, and recordkeeping outside the app.

Mac workflows

Use FixLog across iPhone, iPad, and Mac for review, exports, reporting, setup, and easier data entry.
