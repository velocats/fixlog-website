Feature List

Everything a small operation needs to keep assets maintained.

FixLog combines asset records, maintenance history, reminders, QR codes, groups, locations, templates, documents, backups, and exports in one focused app.

Asset Records

Track name, asset ID, status, category, location, photo, vendor, manufacturer, model, serial number, notes, and dates.

Locations

Use locations like Kitchen, Packaging Area, Boiler Room, Truck 2, Taproom Bar, or Storage Room to find assets quickly.

Asset Groups

Create flexible groups such as Important Tools, Expensive Assets, Safety Critical, Warranty Watch, or Seasonal Equipment.

Maintenance History

Log inspections, cleaning, preventive maintenance, repairs, replacements, costs, parts, labor time, notes, and photos.

Maintenance Reminders

Track overdue, due today, due soon, upcoming, recurring, and completed maintenance work.

Templates and Checklists

Create reusable maintenance templates with checklist steps for repeatable work.

QR Codes

Generate and scan QR codes so staff can open an asset record from the equipment itself.

Documents

Attach photos and PDF documents such as manuals, invoices, inspection forms, and warranty records.

Reports and Exports

Export asset reports, CSV files, QR labels, and FixLog backup files.

Example Company

Explore a fully populated sample company with assets, reminders, templates, maintenance actions, groups, and locations.

Privacy First

No account is required. Your data stays on your device unless you export or share it.
