Feature List

Track maintenance, costs, warranties, and spaces in one private app.

FixLog now supports home and business setup paths, multiple Spaces, cost tracking charts, stronger reminders, asset photos, documents, backups, and storage management.

Built for homes, businesses, rentals, workshops, and everything between.

Start with a home or business setup, then create separate Spaces for each property, shop, cabin, rental, RV, or organization you manage.

Home or Business Setup

Choose a setup direction that fits how you plan to use FixLog, whether you are maintaining a home, business, rental property, workshop, RV, or multiple Spaces at once.

Multiple Spaces

Create separate Spaces such as Main Home, Maple Ridge Home, Cabin, Rental Property, Workshop, RV, or Business. Each Space keeps its own assets, reminders, records, documents, and history separate.

Asset tracking that works for real life.

Keep the practical details you need when something breaks, needs service, or has to be replaced.

Asset Records

Track appliances, tools, vehicles, rooms, home systems, business equipment, and high-value items with status, category, location, vendor, manufacturer, model, serial number, notes, and dates.

Up to 10 Photos Per Asset

Add multiple photos for labels, serial plates, before-and-after work, receipts, installations, parts, and visual documentation. New assets clearly explain when to save before adding photos.

Documents

Attach photos and PDF documents such as manuals, receipts, contractor invoices, inspection forms, warranty records, and repair notes.

Warranty Countdown

See warranty timing from Assets so you can spot items nearing expiration before a repair or replacement becomes more expensive.

Locations and Groups

Use locations like Kitchen, Garage, Utility Room, Basement, Workshop, Packaging Area, Truck 2, Taproom Bar, or Storage Room, plus groups like Warranty Watch, Seasonal Equipment, Safety Critical, and High-Value Items.

Long History Support

Maintenance entries appear higher on the Asset screen, with longer histories collapsed after the most recent three so important records stay useful without overwhelming the page.

Cost tracking and charts.

Understand where maintenance money is going across Spaces, locations, vendors, asset types, and time.

Spend Trends

Use cost charts to see maintenance spend over time across your Spaces, locations, vendors, and maintenance records.

Space and Location Filters

Filter Cost Tracking by Space, then choose a matching Location to narrow the charts to a home, business, workshop, room, vehicle area, or property.

Vendor and Type Breakdowns

Compare costs by vendor, maintenance type, Space, Location, asset value, and replacement exposure.

Asset Value and Replacement Exposure

Track purchase cost and replacement cost so FixLog can help you understand the value of what you maintain and what it may cost to replace.

Multi-Year History

Keep long-term maintenance and cost history so trends, repeat repairs, and replacement planning stay easy to review over time.

Reports and Exports

Export asset reports, CSV files, QR labels, and FixLog backup files for documentation, planning, or recordkeeping.

Reminders, completion, and history.

FixLog is designed to make recurring maintenance easier to finish and easier to remember later.

Maintenance Reminders

Track recurring home and business tasks such as HVAC filters, water heater flushing, gutter cleaning, inspections, safety checks, sanitation, and service work.

Visible Complete Buttons

Complete reminders faster with clearer Complete actions and improved workflows for due and upcoming work.

Multi-Select on Due

Select multiple reminders on the Due screen to complete or delete them together when several tasks are handled at once.

Completion Logging

Completed reminders can automatically create maintenance history entries by default, so work does not disappear after it is marked done.

Upcoming Work

The Home screen now shows the next three non-overdue reminders in Upcoming, even when they are farther out than today.

Templates and Checklists

Create reusable maintenance templates with checklist steps for repeatable work.

Backup, storage, purchase, and access.

Keep FixLog practical as your records grow.

Default Backup Reminder

FixLog includes a default FixLog Backup asset and a weekly backup reminder to encourage regular backup habits.

Storage Management

Settings includes storage management for finding large images and documents when you need to clean up space.

Privacy First

No account is required. Your data stays on your device unless you export or share it.

QR Codes

Generate and scan QR codes so you, family members, helpers, or staff can open a record from the item itself. Learn the QR workflow.

One-Time Pro Unlock

Unlock FixLog Pro with a one-time purchase. Purchase access is available from Settings.

Polished Editing Flow

Several modal and editing screens now use larger presentation sizes, making setup and record updates more comfortable.

Data & Export

Your records should stay useful.

FixLog is designed around practical record ownership: keep Space-by-Space maintenance history in the app, attach supporting documents, export CSV/report files when needed, and create backup files before major changes or device moves.

Simple Setup

Built for owners, not implementation projects.

Use FixLog for the things you already maintain: your home, cabin, rental property, workshop, vehicles, tools, business equipment, and high-value items. Start small, then add Spaces, templates, QR labels, groups, reminders, cost records, and reports as your needs grow.
